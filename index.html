05 / A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Your questions,
considered.

Who is responsible for managing the property?

Property management is handled by a professional management company—our long-term partner with experience in the hospitality and property management sectors.

The company takes full responsibility for the operational management of the property: its maintenance and upkeep, guest services, tenant recruitment, and property marketing. The management company also collaborates with tour operators and other partners to ensure a steady flow of guests and optimal occupancy rates for the residences.

As a result, the owner does not need to personally search for tenants, arrange guest accommodations, or handle the day-to-day management of the property—a professional team takes care of all these matters.

What form of ownership is provided for?

Leasehold terms depend on the project phase. Phase 1 offers 23+ years, with a guaranteed 25-year leasehold extension at a fixed price. Phase 2 offers 80 years. This grants owners a long-term right to use the property and supports long-term ownership planning.

What infrastructure and facilities are included in the project?

The following are planned for the site:

  • 4 restaurants with an atmosphere conducive to relaxation and socializing
  • Bali’s only artificial surf wave for surfing
  • An open-air movie theater under the stars
  • A state-of-the-art fitness center
  • A wellness area for relaxation and rejuvenation
  • An elegant wedding hall for special events
  • An architectural bridge that will become one of the project’s most recognizable visual features

All of this creates a space where everyday life blends with relaxation, activity, and premium service.

How far is the project from the ocean?

The project is located just 2 minutes from the ocean, providing quick access to the coast and the natural beauty of southern Bali.

Can owners live in their residences themselves, or are they intended exclusively for rental?

Yes, owners can live in their residences themselves whenever it is convenient for them. During periods when the owner is not using the property, our management company can handle the rental and guest accommodations to ensure the residence remains in demand and generates income.

What documents are required to purchase a property?

A valid passport is the only document required to begin the property purchase process.

We guide clients through every stage of the transaction and assist with preparing and completing all necessary documentation.

BENEATH THE SURFACE

Technical details.

Lightweight steel frame

Our construction approach uses lightweight steel framing (LGSF), formed from cold-formed steel profiles with stiffening ribs.

Climate performance

Steel framing and a perforated thermal profile are selected to address decay, termite exposure, condensation and thermal bridging in Bali’s climate.

Acoustic comfort

Our published specification lists an Rw sound-insulation rating of 62–68 dB for assemblies using ECO concrete and gypsum fibreboard. Ask for the assembly details and test documentation.

Corrosion protection

The published system uses galvanised steel with 275–350 g/m² of zinc coating, with an optional zinc-aluminium-magnesium (ZAM) composition.

Seismic consideration

Site-specific structural design must be assessed by qualified engineers. Project documentation provides the applicable engineering details.
