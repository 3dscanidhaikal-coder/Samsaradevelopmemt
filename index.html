06 / A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Your questions,
considered.

Who will be responsible for property management and maintenance?

Contact our team for the proposed management provider, maintenance services and fees for your chosen residence. These arrangements will be set out in the project’s management agreement.

What is the ownership structure?

Ask our team for the ownership structure and contract terms available for your chosen residence. The project documents will confirm the tenure, duration and any renewal provisions.

What infrastructure and facilities are included in the project?

Solvyn City’s published plans include villas and apartments, a surf wave, a wellness cluster and event spaces. Facilities and delivery stages should be confirmed against the current project specification.

How far is the project from the ocean?

The published Solvyn City location guide indicates approximately two minutes from the ocean. Explore the map above for the distance to Pandawa Beach and open driving directions to check your route. Travel times vary with traffic and access.

Can owners live in their residences themselves, or are they intended exclusively for rental?

Please discuss your intended use with our team. Personal stays, rental participation and any occupancy conditions should be confirmed in the agreement for your chosen residence.

What documents are required to purchase a property?

Request a purchase-document checklist from our team for your circumstances and chosen ownership structure. The project’s notary or legal adviser can confirm the exact requirements before you proceed.

BENEATH THE SURFACE

Technical details.

Lightweight steel frame

Our construction approach uses lightweight steel framing (LGSF), formed from cold-formed steel profiles with stiffening ribs.

Climate performance

Steel framing and a perforated thermal profile are selected to address decay, termite exposure, condensation and thermal bridging in Bali’s climate.

Acoustic comfort

Our published specification lists an Rw sound-insulation rating of 62–68 dB for assemblies using ECO concrete and gypsum fibreboard. Ask for the assembly details and test documentation.

Corrosion protection

The published system uses galvanised steel with 275–350 g/m² of zinc coating, with an optional zinc-aluminium-magnesium (ZAM) composition.

Seismic consideration

Site-specific structural design must be assessed by qualified engineers. Project documentation provides the applicable engineering details.
